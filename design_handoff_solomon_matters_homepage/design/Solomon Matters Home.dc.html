04 — FAQ

Questions we get first

Do you install the equipment yourself?+

No — and that is deliberate. We design and specify; you hire whoever you like to install, including yourself. Because we do not sell labour or hardware, nothing in the plan exists to fill our calendar.

Is this only for whole-house projects?+

Most of our work is one room at a time. A single consultation on the kitchen is a perfectly normal engagement — we just make sure that room fits a plan the rest of the house can grow into.

Do I have to replace what I already own?+

Rarely. We start from an inventory of your current devices and keep everything that still earns its place. Replacements get a reason written next to them.

What does a consultation cost?+

A flat fee, quoted before we start and based on the size of the home and the number of rooms in scope. Second opinions on an existing quote are a fixed price.

Will it still work if a company shuts down?+

We favour open standards and local control, so lights, locks and climate keep working without an internet connection or a subscription. Where a cloud service is unavoidable, we say so in writing.
